Open a PowerPoint deck in bento/slides

Turn a .pptx into a bento/slides deck: one HTML file that edits and presents itself in any browser, with nothing to install.

🔒Your file never leaves this device. Conversion runs entirely in this browser tab. Nothing is uploaded, and this page runs no analytics. The only things it downloads are the latest bento/slides app and its signed release record, so that the app it puts your deck into is verified as genuine.

What comes across: text, shapes, colours, pictures, where everything sits on each slide, and your speaker notes. What doesn't yet: tables and charts are marked with a placeholder box, and animations are not carried. The list after converting says exactly what changed in your deck.
